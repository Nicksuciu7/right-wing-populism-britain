# Dissertation

**Nicholas Suciu. _Machine Learning - Measuring and Predicting Right-Wing Populism in Britain Since the 2008 Financial Crisis_. BSc Computer Science, University of Sussex, 2026. Supervisor: Dr Maxine Sherman.**

[Read the public reading edition](dissertation-public.pdf).

This edition preserves the submitted research text, results, figures and bibliography. The candidate identifier is removed from the title page; the original proposal and signed administrative ethics appendix are omitted. PDF metadata and annotations are removed. The contents page still describes the full submitted document. These privacy edits do not correct or replace academic claims. The original submission remains outside the public repository.

The dissertation's statement of originality permits copying and distribution with acknowledgement of the source. This permission is separate from the software's MIT licence and does not license BSA survey data.

The original proposal described logistic regression and a broader time range. The completed work uses ridge regression on five waves from 2011–2024. Repository descriptions follow the completed study. Later audit findings and the correction profile are documented in [the audit report](../docs/audit.md), not inserted into the submitted text.
